Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZDubLee's Airside Companion ("Airside Companion," "the application," "we," "our," or "us") accesses, collects, uses, stores, processes, and shares information when you use the application or visit this website.

ZDubLee's Airside Companion is primarily a desktop companion application for flight simulation, virtual air traffic control, livestreaming, and OBS overlays. Connecting Google, YouTube, or Twitch accounts is optional and is only required for features associated with those services.

1. Information Processed by the Application

Airside Companion may process information necessary to provide its flight simulation, virtual ATC, streaming, and overlay features.

This may include:

  • Airport identifiers and airport information
  • Active runway information
  • ATC frequencies
  • Flight simulation session information
  • Aircraft and traffic information
  • Weather and ATIS information
  • Simulator data from supported interfaces
  • Application settings and overlay preferences
  • OBS overlay configuration
  • Authorized YouTube livestream information
  • Authorized Twitch livestream information

2. Google Sign-In and YouTube API Services

Airside Companion uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services to allow users to optionally connect their Google and YouTube accounts.

Airside Companion does not request, collect, or store your Google or YouTube password. Authentication is performed through Google's authorization process.

Access is limited to permissions authorized by the user.

3. Google and YouTube Data We May Access

Depending on the enabled features, permissions granted, account eligibility, and information made available through YouTube API Services, Airside Companion may access:

  • Basic account information used for authentication
  • YouTube channel identification and information
  • Supported channel statistics
  • YouTube livestream information
  • YouTube livestream chat messages
  • Public information associated with chat participants
  • Super Chat and Super Sticker information
  • Channel membership information when supported
  • Other authorized YouTube livestream information necessary to provide an enabled feature

4. How YouTube Data Is Used

Authorized YouTube information may be used to:

  • Connect the user's YouTube account
  • Identify the authorized YouTube channel
  • Identify supported live broadcasts
  • Display live chat
  • Display supported channel information
  • Display Super Chats or Super Stickers
  • Display supported membership activity
  • Provide livestream alerts and tools
  • Display information selected by the user through OBS-compatible overlays

5. Twitch Authentication and API Services

Airside Companion uses Twitch OAuth and Twitch API Services to allow users to optionally connect their Twitch accounts.

Airside Companion does not request, collect, or store your Twitch password. Authentication is performed through Twitch's authorization system.

Access is limited to the permissions authorized by the user.

6. Twitch Data We May Access

Depending on the Twitch features enabled, permissions granted by the user, account eligibility, and information made available through Twitch APIs, Airside Companion may access:

  • Twitch account and channel identification
  • Public channel information
  • Livestream status and broadcast information
  • Twitch chat information when supported
  • Public information associated with chat participants
  • Supported follower, subscriber, viewer, or engagement events when available and authorized
  • Other Twitch information necessary to provide an enabled streaming feature

7. How Twitch Data Is Used

Authorized Twitch information may be used to:

  • Connect the user's Twitch account
  • Identify the authorized Twitch channel
  • Identify active livestreams
  • Display supported Twitch chat information
  • Display supported channel activity
  • Provide livestream alerts or tools
  • Display information selected by the user through OBS-compatible overlays

8. Storage of Streaming Account Data

Airside Companion is designed primarily as a desktop application and processes simulator and streaming information on the user's computer.

OAuth authorization information, such as access or refresh tokens necessary to maintain an authorized connection, may be stored locally by the application on the user's device.

Authorization credentials are used only to communicate with the applicable authorized service.

Livestream information such as chat messages and related live events is intended to be processed for live application functionality and is not intentionally maintained as a permanent remote archive by Airside Companion.

9. Sharing of Streaming Data

Airside Companion does not sell Google, YouTube, Twitch, or other streaming-platform user data.

Airside Companion does not share authorized streaming account data with advertisers or data brokers.

Authorized information is used only to provide the user-facing features described in this Privacy Policy, except where disclosure is required by law.

OBS and livestream disclosure:

Airside Companion allows users to intentionally display selected information through OBS overlays.

If a user enables an overlay containing YouTube or Twitch information, information such as usernames, chat messages, channel activity, Super Chats, memberships, or other supported streaming events may become visible to viewers of the user's livestream or recording.

This public display occurs only when the user chooses to enable and broadcast that information through their streaming setup.

10. Google API Limited Use

Airside Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to applicable Google API Services User Data Policy requirements, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Google and YouTube user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing functionality associated with Airside Companion.

11. Revoking Google and YouTube Access

Users may disconnect their Google or YouTube account from Airside Companion at any time.

Users may also revoke application access through Google's account permissions:

Google Account Connections

Revoking authorization prevents future authorized requests for that Google or YouTube account.

12. Revoking Twitch Access

Users may disconnect their Twitch account from Airside Companion at any time.

Users may also manage or revoke connected applications through their Twitch account settings.

Revoking Twitch authorization prevents Airside Companion from making future authorized requests for that Twitch account.

13. Third-Party Policies

Airside Companion uses YouTube API Services. Users of YouTube-related functionality are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

Google's handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy .

Users of Twitch-related functionality are also subject to the Twitch Terms of Service .

Twitch's handling of information is governed by the Twitch Privacy Notice .

14. Personal Information

Airside Companion does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as financial account credentials, government identification numbers, Google passwords, YouTube passwords, or Twitch passwords.

Information visible through streaming APIs may include account, channel, or profile information made available by the user or streaming platform.

15. Website Hosting and Cookies

The Airside Companion website does not currently use its own advertising cookies or behavioral advertising trackers.

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information according to GitHub's own policies.

16. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information handled by Airside Companion.

OAuth credentials are not intentionally exposed through OBS overlays or publicly displayed by the application.

No computer system or online service can guarantee absolute security.

17. Children's Privacy

Airside Companion is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Airside Companion adds or changes features, APIs, integrations, or data-handling practices.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

19. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the project's GitHub repository or Discord community.
