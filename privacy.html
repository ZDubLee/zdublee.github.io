Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZDubLee's Airside Companion ("Airside Companion," "the application," "we," "our," or "us") accesses, collects, uses, stores, processes, and shares information when you use the application or visit this website.

ZDubLee's Airside Companion is primarily a desktop companion application for flight simulation, virtual air traffic control, livestreaming, and OBS overlays. Connecting a Google or YouTube account is optional and is only required for Google and YouTube-related streaming features.

1. Information Processed by the Application

Airside Companion may process information necessary to provide its flight simulation, virtual ATC, and streaming features.

This may include:

  • Airport identifiers and airport information
  • Active runway information
  • ATC frequencies
  • Flight simulation session information
  • Aircraft and traffic information
  • Weather and ATIS information
  • Simulator data from supported interfaces
  • Application settings and overlay preferences
  • OBS overlay configuration

This information is used to provide the functionality requested by the user.

2. Google Sign-In and YouTube API Services

Airside Companion uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services to allow users to optionally connect their Google and YouTube accounts.

Google authentication is used to securely authorize access to supported YouTube features. Airside Companion does not request, collect, or store your Google or YouTube password.

Access is limited to the permissions authorized by the user through Google's OAuth consent process.

3. Google and YouTube User Data We May Access

Depending on the YouTube features enabled by the user, permissions granted, account eligibility, and data made available through YouTube API Services, Airside Companion may access:

  • Basic Google account information necessary to identify the authenticated user
  • YouTube channel identification and channel information
  • YouTube channel statistics and subscriber information made available through authorized YouTube APIs
  • Active or scheduled YouTube livestream information
  • YouTube livestream chat messages
  • Public information associated with livestream chat participants
  • Super Chat and Super Sticker information
  • YouTube channel membership information when supported and authorized
  • Other YouTube livestream information required to provide a feature explicitly enabled by the user

Airside Companion only requests access required for the YouTube functionality offered by the application.

4. How Google and YouTube Data Is Used

Google and YouTube user data is used only to provide features requested by the authorizing user.

These purposes may include:

  • Connecting the user's YouTube account to Airside Companion
  • Identifying the user's authorized YouTube channel
  • Identifying supported live broadcasts
  • Displaying YouTube live chat within the application
  • Displaying supported channel or subscriber information
  • Displaying Super Chats and Super Stickers
  • Displaying supported channel membership activity
  • Providing streaming alerts and related livestream tools
  • Displaying information selected by the user in OBS-compatible overlays

Google and YouTube user data is not used for unrelated purposes.

5. Storage of Google and YouTube Data

Airside Companion is designed primarily as a desktop application and processes simulator and streaming information on the user's computer.

Google OAuth authorization information, such as access or refresh tokens necessary to maintain an authorized account connection, may be stored locally by the application on the user's device.

Authorization credentials are used only to communicate with authorized Google and YouTube API Services.

YouTube livestream information such as live chat, Super Chats, memberships, and related live activity is intended to be processed for the application's live features and is not retained longer than necessary to provide those features.

Airside Companion does not intentionally maintain permanent remote archives of a user's YouTube live chat, Super Chat, membership, or livestream activity.

6. Sharing of Google and YouTube Data

Airside Companion does not sell Google or YouTube user data.

Airside Companion does not share Google or YouTube user data with advertisers or data brokers.

Authorized data is used by Airside Companion only to provide the features described in this Privacy Policy.

OBS and livestream disclosure:

Airside Companion allows users to intentionally display selected livestream information through OBS overlays.

If a user enables an overlay containing information such as YouTube chat messages, usernames, Super Chats, membership activity, or other supported YouTube events, that information may become visible to viewers of the user's livestream or recording.

This disclosure occurs only when the user chooses to enable and broadcast that information through their streaming setup.

7. Limited Use of Google User Data

Airside Companion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to applicable Google API Services User Data Policy requirements, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Google and YouTube user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing functionality that is prominent and appropriate within Airside Companion.

8. Revoking Google and YouTube Access

Users may disconnect their Google or YouTube account from Airside Companion at any time.

Users may also revoke Airside Companion's access through Google's account security permissions page:

Google Account Permissions

Revoking authorization prevents Airside Companion from making future authorized requests for that Google or YouTube account.

When authorization is revoked, stored authorization credentials and Google or YouTube Authorized Data associated with that authorization will be deleted as soon as reasonably possible and no later than seven calendar days after revocation, as required by applicable YouTube API policies.

9. Google and YouTube Policies

Airside Companion uses YouTube API Services.

By using YouTube-related functionality, users are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

Information handled by Google is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy .

10. Personal Information

Airside Companion does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or Google account passwords.

Information visible through YouTube APIs may include account or channel information that a user or viewer has made available through YouTube.

11. Cookies and Website Analytics

The Airside Companion website does not currently use its own advertising cookies or behavioral advertising trackers.

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information in accordance with GitHub's own policies.

12. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect data handled by Airside Companion.

Google and YouTube authorization credentials are not intentionally exposed through OBS overlays or publicly displayed by the application.

No computer system or online service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Children's Privacy

Airside Companion is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Airside Companion adds or changes features, APIs, integrations, or data-handling practices.

Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
