Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Airside Companion Overlay ("Airside Companion," "the application," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the application or visit its website.

Airside Companion Overlay is designed primarily as a local companion application for flight simulation and ATC streaming. Simulator and session data processed by the companion application is intended to remain on the user's computer unless a feature specifically requires communication with an external service.

1. Information We Collect

Airside Companion Overlay may process information necessary to provide its flight simulation and streaming features. This may include:

  • Airport identifiers and airport information
  • Active runway information
  • ATC frequencies
  • Flight simulation session information
  • Aircraft and traffic information
  • Weather and ATIS information when supported
  • Simulator data made available through supported simulator interfaces
  • Application configuration and overlay preferences

This information is used to provide features such as airport status, ATC information, traffic displays, radar-style overlays, and OBS browser overlays.

2. Personal Information

Airside Companion Overlay does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or precise personal location.

The application does not require users to provide personal information solely to use its core local overlay functionality unless a future service or integration specifically requires such information.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Airside Companion Overlay may be used to:

  • Display real-time flight simulation and ATC information
  • Generate OBS-compatible browser overlays
  • Display airport, runway, frequency, weather, ATIS, and traffic information
  • Provide tower, ground, or radar-style displays when supported
  • Remember application preferences and configuration
  • Diagnose application errors and improve reliability
  • Support new application features and integrations

4. Local Data Processing

Whenever possible, simulator and application data is processed locally on the user's computer.

Local simulator information is not sold and is not intentionally transmitted to third parties unless transmission is required for a feature requested by the user, such as obtaining information from an external weather, aviation, authentication, or data service.

5. Sharing of Information

Airside Companion Overlay does not sell user data.

Information may be shared with third-party services only when necessary to operate a feature of the application. Examples may include aviation weather services, flight simulation services, authentication providers, hosting providers, or other APIs integrated with Airside Companion.

When a third-party service is used, information transmitted to that service may be processed according to that provider's own privacy policy.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

6. Website Hosting and GitHub Pages

The Airside Companion Overlay website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may automatically receive technical information when visitors access this website, such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, and request logs.

This information is handled by GitHub according to GitHub's own privacy policies and is not directly controlled by Airside Companion Overlay.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Airside Companion Overlay does not currently use its own advertising cookies or tracking cookies on this website.

If analytics, authentication, advertising, or other tracking technologies are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is collected and how it is used.

8. Data Retention

Data processed locally by the application remains on the user's device according to the application's configuration and storage requirements.

Airside Companion Overlay does not intentionally retain simulator session information on remote servers unless a feature specifically requires remote storage.

9. Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information handled by Airside Companion Overlay. However, no computer system, network connection, or online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Airside Companion Overlay is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Airside Companion Overlay develops or adds new features, services, APIs, or integrations.

Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the Airside Companion Overlay GitHub repository.
