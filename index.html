Airside Companion Overlay

Real-Time ATC Information for Your Flight Simulation Stream

Airside Companion Overlay is a companion application designed for Airside ATC and Microsoft Flight Simulator. It provides clean, stream-ready airport information through transparent browser overlays that can be added directly to OBS.

Airport Information

Display the active airport, runways, frequencies, session information, and other important ATC data.

OBS Overlays

Purpose-built browser source overlays designed to fit naturally into flight simulation and ATC livestreams.

Live Data

Automatically update overlay information as your Airside session and simulator environment change.

ATC Tools

Built to expand with weather, ATIS, traffic monitoring, tower radar, ground radar, and additional controller tools.
