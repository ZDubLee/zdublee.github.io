ZDubLee's Airside Companion

ATC Companion • YouTube & Twitch Integration • OBS Overlays

ZDubLee's Airside Companion is a desktop companion application for flight simulation, virtual air traffic control, livestreaming, and content creation.

The application works alongside Airside ATC and Microsoft Flight Simulator to provide useful airport, simulator, traffic, weather, ATC, YouTube, Twitch, and streaming information through a companion interface and OBS-compatible browser overlays.

Built for Virtual Air Traffic Control

Airside Companion brings simulator, airport, controller, and livestream information together in one stream-friendly application.

Airport Information

View airport information, active runways, frequencies, session information, and other useful virtual ATC data.

OBS Browser Overlays

Display selected information through browser-source overlays designed for OBS and flight simulation livestreams.

Live Simulator Data

Update supported airport, aircraft, traffic, and session information as the simulator environment changes.

Weather & ATIS

Display supported airport weather and ATIS information for the active virtual ATC session.

Tower Radar

Radar-style traffic displays provide additional situational awareness for virtual controllers.

Ground Radar

View supported aircraft movement on and around airport surfaces in a dedicated ground display.

YouTube Integration

Connect an authorized YouTube account to use supported livestream information, chat, Super Chats, memberships, channel information, and streaming overlays.

Twitch Integration

Connect an authorized Twitch account to use supported channel, livestream, chat, and engagement information within the companion application and OBS overlays.

YouTube & Twitch Integration

Connecting a YouTube or Twitch account is optional. Airside Companion's core flight simulation and virtual ATC functionality does not require a streaming account connection.

Google & YouTube

When a user chooses to connect YouTube, ZDubLee's Airside Companion uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services to identify the authorized YouTube channel and provide supported livestream functionality.

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted by the user, this may include livestream information, live chat, channel information, supported subscriber or channel statistics, Super Chats, Super Stickers, memberships, and related livestream activity.

Twitch

When a user chooses to connect Twitch, ZDubLee's Airside Companion uses Twitch OAuth and Twitch API Services to identify the authorized Twitch account and provide supported livestream functionality.

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted by the user, this may include Twitch channel information, livestream status, chat, and supported viewer or engagement events made available through Twitch.

Streaming-service information is used only to provide user-facing livestream tools and, when selected by the user, OBS-compatible overlays.

Users can disconnect their connected accounts and revoke authorization at any time.

For complete information about how account and streaming data is handled, review our Privacy Policy .

For Simulation Use Only

ZDubLee's Airside Companion is designed exclusively for flight simulation, virtual air traffic control, livestreaming, and entertainment. It is not intended or approved for real-world aircraft navigation, dispatch, weather decisions, or air traffic control.
