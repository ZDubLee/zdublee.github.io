Airside Companion Overlay

Real-Time ATC Information for Your Flight Simulation Stream

Airside Companion Overlay is a companion application designed for Airside ATC and Microsoft Flight Simulator. It provides clean, stream-ready airport information through browser-based overlays that can be added directly to OBS.

Built for the Virtual Controller

Important ATC and simulator information presented in a clean, stream-friendly format.

Airport Information

Display airport information, active runways, frequencies, session details, and other useful controller information.

OBS Browser Overlays

Purpose-built browser source overlays designed to fit naturally into ATC and flight simulation livestreams.

Live Data

Automatically update information as your simulator and Airside session change.

Weather & ATIS

Designed to support useful airport weather and ATIS information directly inside the companion experience.

Tower Radar

Radar-style traffic displays designed to give controllers an improved view of aircraft operating around the airport.

Ground Radar

Ground traffic visualization designed to help monitor aircraft movement across airport surfaces.

Development in Progress

Airside Companion Overlay is actively being developed. Features, integrations, overlay options, and controller tools will continue to expand as the application evolves.
