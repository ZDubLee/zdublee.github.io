ZDubLee's Airside Companion

ATC Companion • YouTube Integration • OBS Overlays

ZDubLee's Airside Companion is a desktop companion application for flight simulation, virtual air traffic control, livestreaming, and content creation.

The application works alongside Airside ATC and Microsoft Flight Simulator to provide useful airport, simulator, traffic, weather, ATC, and streaming information through a companion interface and OBS-compatible browser overlays.

Built for Virtual Air Traffic Control

Airport Information

Google & YouTube Integration

Users may optionally connect their Google and YouTube accounts to ZDubLee's Airside Companion. Connecting a Google account is not required for the application's core flight simulation and virtual ATC features.

When a user chooses to connect YouTube, ZDubLee's Airside Companion uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services to identify the authorized YouTube channel and provide supported livestream features.

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted by the user, these features may include displaying YouTube livestream information, live chat, channel information, subscriber or channel statistics made available by YouTube, Super Chats, Super Stickers, channel memberships, and related livestream activity.

This information is used to provide livestream tools within Airside Companion and, when the user chooses, to display selected information through OBS overlays during their livestream.

Airside Companion only requests Google and YouTube permissions needed for the features enabled by the user. Users can disconnect their account and revoke access at any time.

For complete information about how Google and YouTube data is handled, please review our Privacy Policy .

For Simulation Use Only

ZDubLee's Airside Companion is designed exclusively for flight simulation, virtual air traffic control, livestreaming, and entertainment. It is not intended or approved for real-world aircraft navigation, dispatch, weather decisions, or air traffic control.
